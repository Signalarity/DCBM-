Historical replay holds observed mining history fixed. Future projections model expected mining intervals, not random block arrivals, miner incentives, censorship, price, or reorgs. Neither is a prediction.

Snapshot provenance & calculation notes

Integer target ratios and maturity calculations use arbitrary-precision arithmetic, with one floor operation per retarget. No cap is mathematically unbounded; the original reference retains its 2,147,483,647-block technical ceiling. A disabled floor permits zero. Custom initial maturity is bounded at activation. Historical data is trusted from the collecting Knots node or the public explorer; the standalone report checks links and the fork checkpoint but does not independently verify header proof of work.

Future retargets use the 2,016-block schedule, observed epoch timestamps at the snapshot boundary, fourfold adjustment limits, compact-target rounding, and the mainnet proof-of-work limit. Long-term hash power compounds smoothly between optional step changes. Block timestamps are deterministic expected intervals, rounded to seconds and constrained by median time past. Maturity rules do not feed back into the hash-power assumptions.

READ THE RESULTS CORRECTLY

A replay, not an activation.

This experiment pretends your chosen maturity rule started at the activation height you select. That is independent of the real RDTS expiry gate. It does not change your node or activate DCBM on a public network.

“730 days” means 105,120 blocks at the ten-minute target. Actual or modeled calendar waits can differ. Existing rewards retain their mining-block assignment on their branch.

Future hash-rate inputs describe assumptions. For example, +50% over five years is converted into a smooth compounded trend; you do not need to enter each retarget. Positive and negative jumps can be added separately.

The report contains public headers and parameters only. It works offline and never stores your node’s RPC credentials.

Get current public blocks

Header links and the XBT checkpoint are checked; proof of work is not independently verified. If the explorer is unavailable, use the snapshot tip button for offline testing.
